About Reverie

Your private memory workspace.

Reverie helps you capture, organize, and revisit important notes, thoughts, reminders, documents, screenshots, and personal context.

Back to Account

What you can do

  • Save memories, notes, and ideas before they get lost.
  • Add reminders and follow-ups from dated tasks.
  • Organize information by people, topics, tags, and entities.
  • Search past context quickly, including uploaded files.
  • Keep your personal workspace private and account-based.

Why Reverie exists

Important thoughts, follow-ups, and context often get scattered across chats, notes, screenshots, and memory. Reverie gives you one private place to save and retrieve them when they matter.

Privacy note

Your memories stay tied to your account. Uploaded files, extracted text, reminders, and profile details are used to provide Reverie features.
