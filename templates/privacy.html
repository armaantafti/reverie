Privacy and Data Use

How Reverie stores memories, screenshots, and account data.

Back to Account

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Hosted Privacy Policy

This is Reverie's hosted Privacy Policy page for the web app and Android app. It is published through the Reverie Render web app at /privacy and should be used as the Privacy Policy URL in Google Play Console and other app distribution settings.

When Reverie's data practices change, this hosted page will be updated in the web app so users can review the latest policy without needing to reinstall the Android app.

Information We Collect

  • Account information, such as the email address used to sign in.
  • If you choose Google sign-in, basic Google profile information such as name, email address, profile picture, and login provider information.
  • Content you add, including notes, reminders, saved memories, tags, people, entities, and due times.
  • Images, screenshots, or documents you upload, plus text extracted from those files by OCR or AI-assisted processing.
  • Voice input audio only if you choose to use voice capture.
  • Calendar event details only if you choose to add or sync reminders to a calendar.
  • Basic technical information needed to keep you signed in and operate the app securely.

How We Use Information

  • To save, organize, and show your memories, notes, and reminders.
  • To search, summarize, categorize, and surface relevant memories.
  • To process uploaded screenshots and documents and extract readable text.
  • To generate a text transcript from voice input when you choose to use the microphone.
  • To create calendar entries from dated reminders when you choose a calendar action.
  • To keep your account data separated from other users.

Voice Input

If you choose to use voice capture, Reverie may request access to your device microphone. Microphone access is used only after you tap the voice input button. Audio is used to generate a text transcript and is not recorded in the background. Temporary audio files are deleted after transcription. The generated transcript may be saved as part of your note only if you choose to save it.

Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in using Google, Reverie may receive basic account information such as your name, email address, and profile picture. This information is used to create and manage your Reverie account and to display your profile information inside the app.

Google Calendar

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Reverie may request permission to create and manage calendar events that you add from Reverie. Google Calendar access is optional and separate from Google Sign-In. Reverie does not access your calendar unless you explicitly connect it. You may disconnect Google Calendar from the app settings at any time.

Service Providers

Reverie uses third-party services to run the app, store account data, host uploaded files, process AI features, transcribe voice input, and support optional calendar integration. These may include Supabase, Render, Google services, and OpenAI. Information is shared with these providers only as needed to provide the app's features.

Data Storage and Security

User data is stored in cloud services used by Reverie. The app uses account-based access controls so users can access only their own saved content. No system can guarantee perfect security, but the app is designed to keep service-role credentials server-side and not expose them to the browser or Android app wrapper. Calendar tokens are kept server-side and are not exposed to the frontend. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Data Retention

Notes, reminders, screenshots, documents, transcripts, and calendar links remain until you delete them or request account deletion. Temporary audio used for transcription is deleted after processing. Google Calendar tokens are deleted when you disconnect Google Calendar or delete your account.

Deleting Data

You can delete individual memories in the app. To request account-level deletion and permanent removal of associated data, visit the Account Deletion page.

Children and Families

Reverie should be used with parent or guardian guidance where applicable. Do not upload sensitive information that you do not want stored or processed by the app.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Reverie changes. The updated date above will reflect the latest version.
