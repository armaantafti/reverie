Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026

Reverie is a private memory workspace for saving notes, reminders, recommendations, screenshots, and related context. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information We Collect

  • Account information, such as the email address used to sign in.
  • Content you add, including notes, reminders, recommendations, tags, people, entities, and due times.
  • Images or screenshots you upload, plus text extracted from those images by OCR or AI-assisted processing.
  • Basic technical information needed to keep you signed in and operate the app securely.

How We Use Information

  • To save and show your memories, notes, reminders, and recommendations.
  • To search, summarize, categorize, and surface relevant memories.
  • To process uploaded screenshots and extract readable text.
  • To keep your account data separated from other users.

Service Providers

Reverie uses third-party services to run the app, store account data, host uploaded files, and process AI features. These may include Supabase, Render, Google services, and OpenAI. Information is shared with these providers only as needed to provide the app's features.

Data Storage and Security

User data is stored in cloud services used by Reverie. The app uses account-based access controls so users can access only their own saved content. No system can guarantee perfect security, but the app is designed to keep service-role credentials server-side and not expose them to the browser or Android app wrapper.

Deleting Data

You can delete individual memories in the app. To request account-level deletion or help removing data, contact the app owner or support contact listed in the Google Play Store listing.

Children and Families

Reverie should be used with parent or guardian guidance where applicable. Do not upload sensitive information that you do not want stored or processed by the app.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Reverie changes. The updated date above will reflect the latest version.
