Account Deletion

Request Permanent Deletion

Use this page to request deletion of your Reverie account and associated data.

Back to Account

Last updated: May 9, 2026

How to Request Deletion

Send an account deletion request from the email address used for your Reverie account to the developer support email listed on Reverie's Google Play Store listing. Use the subject line Delete my Reverie account.

Include the Reverie login email address in the message so the account can be identified. Do not include passwords or sensitive identity documents in the request email.

What Will Be Deleted

  • Your Reverie account profile associated with the requested email address.
  • Saved memories, notes, reminders, recommendations, tags, people, entities, and due times.
  • Uploaded screenshots, images, documents, extracted text, and generated summaries linked to the account.

What May Be Retained Temporarily

Limited operational records may be retained for a short period where required for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or backup recovery. These records are not used to provide the app experience after deletion.

Timing

Deletion requests are reviewed and processed as soon as reasonably possible. You may be asked to verify that you control the account email before permanent deletion is completed.

Before You Request

You can delete individual memories and uploaded files inside the app. Account deletion is permanent and removes the account-level data listed above.
